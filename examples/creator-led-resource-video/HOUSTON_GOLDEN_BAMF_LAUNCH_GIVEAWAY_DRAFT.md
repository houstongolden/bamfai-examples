## Production and approval gates

1. Use only Houston's approved likeness reference and authorized cloned voice. Keep identity, wardrobe, lighting, and camera continuity stable across founder shots.
2. Generate a short founder talking sample first; reject identity drift, uncanny motion, or voice mismatch. A clean founder voiceover over real footage is the fallback.
3. Use no fabricated BAMF interface. Treat holographic coworkers as metaphorical animation, not product evidence. Capture any specific UI claim from the live app.
4. Do not copy the reference creator's voice, wording, footage, music, or distinctive edit. The exact Reel has not been analyzed.
5. Render privately; watch the complete export for likeness, speech sync, audio, captions, CTA, and factual accuracy before any publication.
