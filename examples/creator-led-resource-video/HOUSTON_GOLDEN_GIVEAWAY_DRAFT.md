- **Offer:** The free, public [Creator-Led Resource Video skill](https://github.com/houstongolden/bamfai-examples/tree/main/examples/creator-led-resource-video). It helps plan an original script, shot list, and quality review for a real product or resource.

**Founder, on camera:** “Start with what you're building, who it's for, and the moment that makes it click.”

**Voiceover over real screen capture:** “We made our creator-led video skill free. It turns that context into an original script, a beat-by-beat shot plan, and a review checklist.”
